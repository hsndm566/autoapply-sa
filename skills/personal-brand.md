# PERSONAL BRAND — inbound is cheaper than outbound

_The engine builds the client's online presence IN PARALLEL to applying. `personal_brand.py` runs weekly._

## Weekly loop
1. Pick ONE topic in the client's field where they have something useful to say.
2. Draft a LinkedIn post/article in the client's **voice** (from profile/CV).
3. **QUEUE for approval** — never auto-post. Client approves + posts.
4. Track engagement (likes/comments/views) + post type -> 90-day pattern.
5. After a post spike, capture **LinkedIn profile viewers** as warm leads.
6. Draft a **connection request** to every recruiter/hiring-manager viewer within 48h.

## Why
Inbound opportunities cost less than outbound volume. A recruruiter who finds YOU is a warmer lead than 100 cold applications. Build both simultaneously.

## Data
- Content calendar + pending-approval posts: this file.
- Engagement log: `### ENGAGEMENT` entries.
- Warm leads (viewers): captured after spikes, connection drafts queued.

## Note
Approval + posting + viewer-monitoring require the client's LinkedIn session (human-gated). The agent prepares everything; the client clicks post + connects.
