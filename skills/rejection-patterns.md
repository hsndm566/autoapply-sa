# REJECTION PATTERNS — self-learning from every no-response

_Living doc. Grows only. Each 10-day-no-response triggers a `diagnose()` entry. Monthly `monthly_rejection_analysis()` appends a pattern report + ONE recommended fix._

## What gets logged per diagnosis
- **Date**, **company**, **role**, **client**
- **Actively hiring?** — derived from public signals (LinkedIn/Glassdoor/Blind scan for freeze/layoff/paused)
- **Instability flag** — lawsuit/scandal/bankrupt/toxic/1-star signals
- **CV/JD mismatch score** out of 10 (10 = perfect match)
- **Signal snippet** — raw search evidence

## Monthly pattern analysis
Runs 1st of month (cron). Counts high-mismatch entries + recurring companies, then recommends ONE specific action (e.g. "add missing keywords X to CV tailoring", "deprioritize company-type Y").

## GOAL
Learn from every rejection automatically. The engine gets smarter with every batch — not just more applications, better-targeted ones.
